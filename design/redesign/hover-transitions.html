Hover open: pick a transition

Hover each minimized tab (it opens after 150ms and closes 300ms after you leave, like the extension). Tab and panel are two glass layers that cross-fade, and every move is a CSS transition: it plays out the same way it came in (exit ease-in, entrance ease-out), the glass itself animates so nothing jumps, and moving back mid-way reverses from where it is. Under reduced motion every option becomes a short fade.
